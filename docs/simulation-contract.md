# Simulation and accounting

See [the pre-evaluation decision record](decisions/001-replay-contract.md) for the authoritative initial date, January convention, execution, fees and pending-order rules. Each portfolio starts with its own $10,000. Initial weights are decided after 2019-12-31 close and executed on 2020-01-02 open. Monthly equal weight next decides after the first session of each later month, for the following open; buy-and-hold keeps units. If there is no eligible next session within the replay, a decision stays pending.

Targets are long-only and sum to at most one. Desired dollar holdings use pre-trade open equity; sell first and then scale buys proportionally to available cash including 5 bps each side. There is no leverage and no cash interest. Equity is units times adjusted close plus cash. Net return uses original capital. Drawdown includes original capital in the running peak. Annualized volatility is sample standard deviation of daily close-to-close portfolio returns, including the first close relative to initial capital, times sqrt(252). Turnover sums absolute trade notional divided by pre-trade open equity, without a half-turnover convention. Cost is total fees.
