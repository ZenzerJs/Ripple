# First replay contract (before evaluation)

Universe SPY, IEF, GLD, VNQ. Each strategy starts independently with $10,000 cash. Use 2019-12-30 for one prior close, decide equal weights after 2019-12-31 close and execute at the 2020-01-02 adjusted open. Buy-and-hold then retains units. Monthly equal weight uses the same initial purchase, with no duplicate January decision; for each subsequent month decide after its first session close and execute at the next session open. Decisions are fixed before seeing that open. If a decision has no next session inside the evaluation, report it as pending, not executed.

Adjusted OHLC equals raw OHLC multiplied by that row's Adj Close / Close factor, from yfinance with auto_adjust=False, actions=True. Units are fractional total-return accounting units, not actual ETF shares. Splits and dividends are embedded in adjusted prices and never added as cash. This adjustment approximates a total-return series, including adjusted opens, and is not point-in-time vendor history. Conservative daily observations become available at midnight New York time following each session. Historical intraday execution is simulated, not a claim of live data availability. Validated XNYS calendar must have exactly one finite, positive, consistent OHLC bar per symbol/session; missing/duplicate sessions are errors.

Target weights must cover known symbols, be finite, nonnegative and sum to at most one. Cash earns zero. At execution, calculate targets from pre-trade open equity; sell first, charging 5 bps of each sell notional, then scale all buys proportionally to available cash including their 5 bps fees. Fractional units allowed; never borrow. Mark equity using adjusted close every evaluation session. Observations contain only bounded close-to-close returns through the decision, never price levels or future bars. Strategy state contains cash and weights, not execution prices. Pending orders are not silently filled beyond end date.

Report net return from initial $10,000 to final close; daily returns include first close relative to the original $10,000; annualized volatility is sample standard deviation of those returns times sqrt(252). Drawdown is equity versus the running peak including the initial $10,000; report most negative drawdown. Turnover is the sum of each trade's absolute notional divided by pre-trade open equity at its execution, without dividing by two. Total costs are the sum of trade fees. Report independent ledger, trades, allocations and equity paths, comparison and reproducible manifest with data/config/source/environment hashes. Historical runs use frozen snapshots only; no fallback or network in replay.
